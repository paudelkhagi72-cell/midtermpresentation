About Nepal

Nepal is a country in South Asia, home to the Himalayas, diverse communities, historic monuments, and unique cultural traditions. From the ancient cities of the Kathmandu Valley to the natural beauty of the mountains and national parks, Nepal offers visitors many unforgettable experiences.
